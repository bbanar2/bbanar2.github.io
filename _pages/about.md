I am a Researcher at <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.eit.org/">Ellison Institute of Technology Oxford</a> (AI & Robotics Institute).

My current work sits at the intersection of Generative AI, AI for Science and Deep Creative Decision-making, pushing beyond the boundaries of both utilitarian and aesthetics-related tasks.

Previously, I conducted research at <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://deepmind.google/">Google DeepMind</a>, <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://ai.sony/">Sony</a> and <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.bose.com/en_us/index.html">Bose</a> on Generative AI for Audio, Music and Speech, and was an Enrichment Student at <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.turing.ac.uk/">The Alan Turing Institute</a>. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="http://eecs.qmul.ac.uk/">Queen Mary University of London</a> (<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.aim.qmul.ac.uk/">AIM CDT</a> & <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/">C4DM</a>).

I am fundamentally interested in the nature of creativity and creative intelligence through the lenses of information theory, optimization and generative modelling.

<summary><strong>Research & Background</strong></summary>

<p>My doctoral research, supervised by <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=L6GPVfcAAAAJ">Simon Colton</a>, focused on "Composing Contemporary Classical Music Using Generative Deep Learning", where I enhanced both machine and human creativity by empowering machines for creative decision-making, while also exploring new aesthetics and challenging entrenched practices in deep learning.</p>

<p>I hold a BS in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://ee.bilkent.edu.tr/en/">Bilkent University</a>, Ankara, Turkey, and a BM in Electronic Production and Design from <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://college.berklee.edu/electronic-production-design">Berklee College of Music</a>, Boston, MA. I’ve also worked at <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="http://users.eecs.northwestern.edu/~aydin/MNDL/Home.html">Northwestern University Metamaterials and Nanophotonic Devices Lab</a> (Nanophotonics Researcher).</p>

<p>I’ve published at the top venues in the field such as ICLR, AAAI, ICASSP, NeurIPS (workshops), EvoMUSART, ICCC, IEEE MMSP and IEEE CAI, and in journals such as Machine Intelligence Research, Applied Physics Letters and Optical Materials Express. I also co-authored a book chapter on Explainable AI for Arts and Music (published by Taylor & Francis).</p>

<p>At Bose, I contributed to a patent, leading efforts on using generative deep learning for audio in augmented hearing scenarios.</p>

<p>My awards include the prestigious Enrichment Community Award (The Alan Turing Institute), Exceptional Great Work Award (Bose), Outstanding Students of 2022 (EvoMUSART), IEEE MMSP Top 10% Paper Award, UKRI CDT in AI and Music Scholarship (QMUL), Roland Award Endowed Scholarship (Berklee), Comprehensive (Full) Scholarship (Bilkent University) and Outstanding Success Scholarship (Turkish Educational Foundation, upon ranking 17th in 1.5 million people in national university entrance exam).</p>

<p>As a musician (drums and electronics), I’ve performed at venues such as the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.mfa.org/">Museum of Fine Arts Boston</a>, <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://harvardplanning.emuseum.com/sites/325/holden-chapel">Harvard University Holden Chapel</a> & <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://carpenter.center/">Carpenter Center for Visual Arts</a> (an original piece premiered as part of <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://college.berklee.edu/focused/interdisciplinary-arts">Berklee Interdisciplinary Arts Institute</a>), <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.berklee.edu/BPC">Berklee Performance Center</a>, <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://wallyscafe.com/">Wally’s Jazz Club Boston</a>, <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://nardisjazz.com/">Nardis Jazz Club Istanbul</a> and <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://caz.iksv.org/en">Istanbul Jazz Festival</a>.</p>
